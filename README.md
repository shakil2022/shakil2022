## 👨‍🏫 About Me

I am a **Lecturer** in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at
**[Bangladesh University of Business and Technology (BUBT)](https://bubt.edu.bd/)**.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning, and Healthcare AI**. I am particularly interested in developing reliable, data-efficient, and privacy-aware intelligent systems for real-world applications.

### 🔬 Research Interests

* 🤖 Machine Learning & Deep Learning
* 🌐 Federated Learning & Federated Semi-Supervised Learning
* 🔐 Privacy-Preserving & Secure Machine Learning
* 🛡️ Cybersecurity & Trustworthy AI
* 🏥 Healthcare AI & Medical Data Analytics
* 👁️ Computer Vision & Image Processing
* 🧠 Large Language Models & Generative AI
* 🧹 Federated Unlearning & Data-Efficient Learning
* 📊 Explainable & Interpretable AI
